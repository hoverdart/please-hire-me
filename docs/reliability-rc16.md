# Verified application quality candidate 0.5.0rc16

The objective is verified, eligible, relevant applications completed. Browser sessions started are diagnostic information and do not count as completed applications.

## Four gates

1. **Eligibility.** Existing degree, graduation, authorization, experience and location checks remain mandatory. Explicit technical prerequisites without confirmed skill evidence require review. Summer-only searches respect the season in the title, including titles such as “Software Intern (2027 Start) - Winter”; body text about a different summer program cannot override it. Numbered experienced engineering levels are excluded.
2. **Relevance.** Being an intern no longer supplies enough points to pass the default fit threshold. Scores require technical overlap with confirmed skills. Generic research preferences do not include quantitative trading/research, clinical or mechanical roles, and “software defined radio hardware” does not establish a software engineering role. Explicit specialist search preferences remain possible. Confirmed experience in matching backend, production, AI, manufacturing-software and research domains adds ranking evidence after eligibility passes. Ranking never approves an answer or overrides a hard gate.
3. **Integrity.** Existing facts, exact documents, scoped approvals and duplicate protections remain mandatory. Missing-information holds depend on relevant facts and exact answers rather than every profile revision. A new model allowance cannot release a form that still has unanswered personal questions. Unchanged legacy holds are narrowed without reopening them. Rejected essays are cached against their source context and wait for supporting material or an exact approval. Fact matching is skipped when every available source would be rejected by the executor.
4. **Confirmation.** A success return from a browser must agree with the durable confirmed application record. Missing evidence requires review and cannot count toward the batch target. No uncertain final submission is retried automatically. Reports include the saved application reference, confirmation time, posting link and immutable resume hash when available.

## Reports and limits

Reports lead with confirmed submissions, human interventions and unconfirmed results. Reviewed owner-bound assessments and interviews include next actions; unreviewed employer messages remain suggestions awaiting review. Latest reviewed stages supersede older stages. Browser-session counts appear in diagnostics. Creating a report does not fetch or send messages; normal configured delivery remains separate.

The worker no longer increases a batch's completion target to catch up with a daily trajectory. Existing submission ceilings and model limits remain enforced. An undersupplied search can finish below its target; it must not widen eligibility to compensate.

Applicants who want only summer internships should select Summer 2027 internships rather than the broader internship option. Part-time school-year work remains a separate choice. Missing facts, agreements, login verification and applicant work samples still need the applicant. These changes reduce wasted work; they do not establish new employer acceptance or guarantee unattended completion. The NVIDIA receipt evidence from [rc15](reliability-rc15.md) remains specific to that application.
